A rest area located among young forest that foresters planted after
the last fire. The intention of the originator was to enable tourists to observe
the process of restoration and growth of forest development, so to speak "from within". Moreover, on
cloudless nights, this is an excellent place for stargazing. Along the path
leading to the platform, several boards dedicated to forest fires are located.
The choice of this location was not accidental. In the last 100 years, the surrounding forests
were destroyed by fires at least five times – most recently this happened on April 25, 2013.
