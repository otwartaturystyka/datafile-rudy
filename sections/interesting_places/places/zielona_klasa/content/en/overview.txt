A wooden shelter built in 2003 through the efforts of the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District
with financial support from the Racibórz County Environmental Protection Fund.
It serves primarily for foresters to conduct classes in natural and forest education
for the public. It is also the main point of the cycling route
"Freedom is in Nature", which was created in 2015. An interesting fact is
that the concrete floor of the "Green Classroom" is already several decades old. Many
years ago, a wooden house was built at this place, which was later
dismantled under circumstances that remain unexplained to this day.
