Two fire lookout towers were built in the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District, of which
the first (this one!) is located in the Borowiec Forest District at the
intersection of forest roads "Kotlarska" and "Barachowska", and the second in the Solarnia
Forest District. The history of this first one is interesting. At the turn of the 1960s and
1970s, the first wooden fire lookout tower was built here,
the remains of which in the form of concrete foundations are visible to this day. In 1998,
using funds from the European "PHARE" fund, a modern, metal
lookout tower was built with a comfortable observation cabin for those times. Unfortunately,
strong winds damaged the structure, which is why a decision was made to build
a new, this time concrete fire tower. It was built in 2017
and measures 43 meters in height, which is 10 meters higher than its predecessor.
