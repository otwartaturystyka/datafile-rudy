It's worth stopping here and looking at the charm of the Ruda River (not "Rudka", as some
people say!). This is often the final stop for kayak trips
flowing from the Rudy side. On hot days, you can cool down at this place by swimming
or just soaking your feet. During the flood in 2010, the bridge foundations were
undermined by water and repairs were necessary, which were carried out by
the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District.
