To provide the fire brigade with access to water, which is necessary for
extinguishing forest fires, after the great fire in 1992, several fire reservoirs were built in the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District.
Two of them are located by the "Kotlarska" road. This is one of them. Unfortunately, due to
the deepening drought over the past few years, their capacity, and thus
usefulness, is decreasing. Nearby there are benches where tired cyclists and runners
can rest.
