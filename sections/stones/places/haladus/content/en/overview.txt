It commemorates a long-time forester of the Jankowice Forest District, Mr. Andrzej Haładus,
who worked for over 40 years in the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District, leaving behind
many forest plantations and young stands, especially beech and fir. Since
July 2018, Mr. Andrzej has been enjoying a well-deserved retirement.

According to unwritten custom, the stone is also accompanied by a table and benches.
