In this place, in the quiet center of the forest, by the road called Barachowska, since 1852
rests a stone called "Ducal". There are several theories about
why it is located here, but it is not known which one is true.

One of them is that someone shot a large animal at this place. However, this is
unlikely, because usually such "tombstones" have a described fact in their content.

Another one says that there was a forester's lodge at this place years ago. However,
the nearest one (Stary Barach) was located as far as 700 meters from
the stone.

So both theories are not entirely true. Therefore, one should look at
the inscriptions carved on the stone. There is a crown, under it a single letter V, and
below H v R and the date - 1852. Looking through the prism of the date, at that time
the owner of these lands was Viktor Moritz 1. Herzog von Ratibor und 1. Fürst
von Corvey. And it is probably his initials and title that are carved on this stone.

However, it is difficult to connect this date with anything...
