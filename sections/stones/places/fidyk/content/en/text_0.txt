Great Fire

In 1992-2002, Forest District Manager Janusz Fidyk was responsible for the development of
forest areas destroyed by the catastrophic fire in August 1992.
The fire then consumed over 9,000 hectares of forest in three forest districts: Rudy
Raciborskie, Rudziniec and Kędzierzyn. This was the largest forest fire in the history
of post-war Europe. The task was very difficult. Currently, several decades after
the catastrophe, it can be seen that it was fully accomplished.
