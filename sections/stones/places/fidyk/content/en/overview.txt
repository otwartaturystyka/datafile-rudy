In the Rudy Forests, there has been a tradition for centuries of commemorating important events or
people deserving of the forest with memorial stones. Such a person without a doubt
is Janusz Fidyk – Forest District Manager of the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District. During
the great forest fire in August 1992, he participated in firefighting operations. After
extinguishing the fire, for the next 10 years he managed the process of rebuilding the burned area. In
2002, he was appointed Forest District Manager in the Rybnik Forest District. In
December 2017, he retired with a well-deserved pension. In 2018, fellow foresters
from the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District, in recognition of his many years of service,
honored the Forest District Manager, as tradition requires, with a stone.
