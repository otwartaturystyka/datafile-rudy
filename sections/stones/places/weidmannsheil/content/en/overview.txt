"Weidmannsheil" in free translation means the same as our "Darz Bór". More
precisely, "Weidmannsheil" is a greeting with the salutation "Successful hunting". And nothing
strange about that, as this stone has been erected here since the times when the
Dukes of Racibórz family ruled these lands, by the forest road behind the Szybki settlement,
which led the route towards Jankowice and to a separated forest district,
which was a game park for breeding and hunting, called Thiergarten.

In the post-war years, when early PRL times came to Poland, a certain forester
turned this historical stone with the carved inscription face down, so it looked like
an ordinary boulder and would not be eliminated. At that time, there was such a trend to destroy everything that was
German in these lands. In this position, it waited for several dozen years.

When the PRL became established, the stone was again placed in the proper position, and later
on a prepared hillock. It was placed on the hillock with the consent of the then
Forest District Manager, but with the proviso that for "political correctness", something similar had to be done in Polish.
And so the "Darz Bór" stone was created, which to this day
can be seen on the right side of the forest road "Kotlarska" right after the railway crossing,
at the beginning of the cycling route "Freedom is in nature".
