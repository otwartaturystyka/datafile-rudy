Thiergarten

"Thiergarten" (a historical name visible only on old maps) was a
19th-century game park of the Dukes of Racibórz (German: Herzog von Ratibor),
arranged in part of the Racibórz forests between Jankowice, Ruda Kozielska and
Kuźnia Raciborska. The family bred magnificent specimens of forest game here, which
were then shot by their distinguished guests. Residents were forbidden to enter.
