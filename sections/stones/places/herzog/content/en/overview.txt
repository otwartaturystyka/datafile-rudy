This stone was erected to commemorate a joint hunt of the Duke of Racibórz
Francis I Albrecht von Ratibor with forester Ryszard Haider.
This event took place on October 1, 1996 (at the time of writing this description,
the text on the stone is already almost completely illegible). During his visit
to Rudy, the Duke asked to go hunting once more in the "Rudy Forests".
"Herzog" in German means "Duke".
