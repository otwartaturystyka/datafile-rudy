This stone commemorates a long-time employee of the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District,
Mr. Józef Szendzielorz, who was known to everyone as "Zep". The stone is
an expression of special respect and gratitude to Mr. Józef, who was
a good, kind, and hardworking man. Mr. Józef died as a result of
illness in May 2010. The stone was erected on the first anniversary of his death in
the place where Mr. Józef worked for the last time.
