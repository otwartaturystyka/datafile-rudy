At this place, on October 1, 1892, the then Duke Viktor von Ratibor
hunted a deer. In memory of this event, a stone was erected (of course with
a German inscription - in those days these were German territories). This was the
duke's last deer, as he died shortly after hunting it, on January 30, 1893.

Today, next to the old stone, there is also a new one - with a Polish inscription.
