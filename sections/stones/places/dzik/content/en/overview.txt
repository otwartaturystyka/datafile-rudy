This stone was erected in memory of a hunt that took place in the Rudy forests
on November 15, 1780. A wild boar weighing 5 centners was hunted then (1
Prussian centner is about 51.5 kg). This place is located by the road called
Zakazana (Forbidden). Its name is related to the fact that once entry to it was forbidden,
because the Duke of Racibórz and his guests did not want witnesses to their hunts.
