The late Józef Ślązak was a long-time forester of the Rudy Raciborskie Forest District in
the Kotlarnia Forest District. In his professional work, he had to face a huge
tragedy – during the great fire in August 1992, his forest district
burned almost entirely. However, thanks to determination and persistent work,
he led to the creation of a new forest. This stone was erected in 2017,
when Mr. Józef retired with a well-deserved pension after 45 years of work. Nearby
is the "Spalony Las" (Burned Forest) Wilderness, where from information boards you can
learn more about forest fires and the revitalization process. There are also
benches and tables there.

Mr. Józef passed away on June 9, 2025.
