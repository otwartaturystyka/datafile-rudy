History

The exact date of construction of the chapel is not known, nevertheless, taking into account its
neo-Gothic style and the date of the figure's creation, it should be assumed that it comes from
around 1860 and was built during the reign of the Duke of Racibórz
Viktor I Maurice von Ratibor. At that time, the buildings of the complex were being rebuilt
in neo-Gothic costume, and the project was prepared by the Wrocław architect
Karl Lüdecke.
