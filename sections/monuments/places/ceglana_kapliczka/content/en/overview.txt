North of the monastery complex, a brick house chapel is built with
a height of 615 cm, width of 302 cm, length of 380 cm, made of clinker brick on
a rectangular plan. This chapel is open to the south towards the monastery
in front of the former "castle pond". The facade is crowned with a high gable,
decorated with arcades, supported by buttresses. In the entrance, ending in a pointed arch,
there is an ornamental grille. In the gable facade, three niches are simulated.
The gable is crowned with a metal cross.
