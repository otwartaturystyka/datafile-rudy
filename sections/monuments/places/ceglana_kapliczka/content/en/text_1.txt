Figure inside

Inside the chapel there is a figure of the Virgin Mary with a height of 172 cm made of
sandstone. It stands on a base that resembles a fragment of an altar in its appearance.
During the conservation of the figure, an inscription was found: "Johan Janda Berlin 1873",
which has enormous historical value because it tells about the year of foundation and the artist
who made it. However, the motive for erecting the chapel is not known.
