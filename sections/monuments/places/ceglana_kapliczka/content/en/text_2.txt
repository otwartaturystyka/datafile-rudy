Worth mentioning is Piotr Siemko's article "Remarks on Upper Silesian iconography",
where the author is guided by F.B. Werner's drawing, which
presents the Rudy abbey and its surroundings from the south "from a bird's eye view". In
this drawing, you can see a road encircling the entire complex, along which there are
chapels. In the mentioned drawing there are five of them, but it is not excluded that there could be
more.
