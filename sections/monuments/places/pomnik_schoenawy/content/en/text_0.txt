Anton Schoenawa

He was born on March 15, 1810. Orphaned early, thanks to the help of a guardian
he completed school in Pawłowiczki, and then higher schools, though it is not known
where. In 1840, he was appointed by the Duke of Racibórz as the manager of both his
ironworks in Kuźnia Raciborska. In 1845, he founded his own ironworks there,
calling it Hoffnungshütte (Hope Ironworks). Compared to other similar plants
existing in the area, this ironworks applied a number of technical innovations and quickly
developed. In 1857, a rolling mill was launched. Rails, wagon axles,
small metal parts and bar iron were produced. In 1847, he married Maria Wilhemina
Schultze, with whom he had twelve children. He died of pneumonia on March 3, 1888.
