The first church in Kuźnia Raciborska was built as early as 1646, that is, 5 years after
the first iron forge, called Segenberger Hammer, was launched here. Above
the church doors was the inscription "Bartholomaus Rumpel 1646". Father Hampel
began construction of a new church in Kuźnia Raciborska in 1900 using fireproof
materials. It was built on the same plot, slightly south of the wooden
church. The consecration of the church in Kuźnia Raciborska took place in 1904.
The patron of the construction of the new church in Kuźnia Raciborska was the Duke of Racibórz.
The temple was consecrated in 1917.
