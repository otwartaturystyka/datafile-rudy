Building history

The historic 19th-century building represents a mixed neo-Romanesque style with
neo-Gothic elements. Its construction was initiated by the court physician of the Duke of Racibórz,
Dr. Juliusz Roger – an outstanding Upper Silesian physician, passionate
ethnographer, and collector of Upper Silesian folk songs published in the form of
a compact collection by the then well-known Wrocław publishing house Skutch in 1863.
The hospital currently serves as the headquarters of the "Cistercian
Landscape Compositions of Rudy Wielkie" Landscape Park. Preserved to this day, the small hospital building
in Rudy is currently the headquarters of the Educational Center of the Landscape Parks Complex
"Cistercian Landscape Compositions of Rudy Wielkie". The center
conducts classes on eco-development, nature protection methods, principles of practicing
tourism without harm to the environment, and the natural, landscape,
historical, and cultural values of the landscape park. The program for schools is implemented
in the form of talks, workshops, festivals, and ecological competitions. In
the program - among others - "Dymarki", "Cistercian Fair", and "Eco-treasure hunt".
Near the hospital building there is a stone commemorating the activities
of Dr. Roger.
