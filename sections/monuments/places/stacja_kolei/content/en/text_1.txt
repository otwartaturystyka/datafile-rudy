Station in Rudy

Almost exactly in the middle of the 51-kilometer route, the station in Rudy Wielkie was located,
the largest on the entire line. There were repair workshops
for rolling stock, sidings for wagons and locomotives, a steam locomotive service point and
a triangular track for turning them. The main station building is a two-story structure
on a rectangular plan with a basement and attic. The multi-pitched roof was covered with
tiles. The floor and attic were originally made of half-timbered construction. The walls
of the ground floor were brick-built with rusticated corners. In the rooms of this
floor were located the station master's office, signal box, waiting room for
travelers, a ticket office serving passenger and freight traffic, and a shelter for
conducting teams. A single-story warehouse
was attached to the southern wall of the building, also made of half-timbered construction covered with a gable roof. Near
the described buildings was a single-edge platform. Parallel to the platform,
opposite the station building, two spacious halls were located, one of which
was intended for locomotives, the other for wagons.
