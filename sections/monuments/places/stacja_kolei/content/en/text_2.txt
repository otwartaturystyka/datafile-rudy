Current state

Today, only a six-kilometer section from Stanica
through Rudy to Paproć and facilities with a track layout in Rudy remain of the former railway. The main station
buildings at the stations in Stanica and Paproć, after the destruction they suffered in
1945, were ineptly rebuilt and lost their former appearance. At the station in Rudy,
the station building was also destroyed, and today's building has only
original cellars. Of the rolling stock halls, only the
locomotive shed remains, but with a new, post-war roof. The wagon hall was
dismantled in 1969. The entire railway line from Gliwice to Racibórz Markowice
was entered in the provincial register of monuments in 1993 and is protected
by law. In this way, the first step was taken to save this extremely
interesting rail transport system, which has a rich, over
hundred-year history, and most importantly – advantages for further use in
its current character for the next decades. Recent attempts to reactivate the entire line of this extraordinary railway since 2009 have been taking place under
the auspices of the Municipal Center for Culture, Sports and Recreation in Kuźnia Raciborska.
They have real chances of success, including the return of steam locomotives to the route.
