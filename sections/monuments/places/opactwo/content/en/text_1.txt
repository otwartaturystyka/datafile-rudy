Renovation

The monastery and palace complex in Rudy consists of a church that is a three-nave
basilica, a quadrangle of the medieval post-Cistercian monastery, and later
added wings of the baroque palace residence. The church part was
restored in 1994-1996. However, the monastery and palace part, being
state property since the post-war period, gradually fell into ruin. On
August 15, 1998, it was transferred to the ownership of the Gliwice diocese.
Since then, gradual work has been carried out there aimed at rehabilitation and
adaptation of the complex for new functional purposes that the facility is to
fulfill.
