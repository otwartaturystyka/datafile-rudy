About the abbey

The origins of the Cistercian Order date back to 1098, when Abbot Robert de Molesmens
left the mother Benedictine monastery in Molesmens, France, and founded a new order
in Dijon, Burgundy, following the original, strict rule of St. Benedict.
The order quickly gained popularity not only in France but also in
other parts of Europe, reaching Jędrzejów around 1140. From there,
in the mid-13th century, the first monks were brought to Rudy. The abbey, although
located within the boundaries of the Wrocław diocese, was established as a branch of Lesser Poland's
Jędrzejów. Prince Władysław III is considered the founder.
