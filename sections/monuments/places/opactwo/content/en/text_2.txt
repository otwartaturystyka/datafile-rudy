Architecture

The former monastery part consists of wings in the form of a quadrangle adjacent
to the church with an inner courtyard and cloisters forming a cloister.
These buildings are two-story, in the western and northern parts
with basements. The former palace residence part consists of
northwestern and northeastern wings. They form an external
courtyard, partially open from the north side, from which entrances lead
to individual parts of the facility. In the opening between these wings is located
the entrance to the courtyard and two external routes, from which there is a
view of the adjacent park pond. The western wing passes into the northern wing,
at the junction of which there is a corner tower. From the courtyard side
to the eastern wall of the western wing, a staircase was added.
These buildings are three-story. Through the western wing
there is a passage, constituting another entrance to the courtyard, this time from the side
of Cysterska Street. The eastern wing is two-story, in the northern part
with a basement. From the east side there is an exit from the building to
the garden area through a terrace. The eastern wing passes into the three-story
northeastern wing, at the junction of which there is a tower that formerly served
as vertical communication. Although the buildings of the monastery and palace complex
have different numbers of floors, the external walls reach
the same height, ending with a common cornice. This was achieved through
differentiation of foundation levels as well as different floor heights in
individual wings. The facades of the building have baroque decoration, although they are
very damaged and mostly devoid of plaster. Only a few fragments have survived
with remnants of baroque decorations.
