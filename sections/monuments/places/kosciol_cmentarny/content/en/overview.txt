The cemetery church of St. Mary Magdalene is located right at the entrance to
Rudy from the Gliwice side, on Dworcowa Street. The current brick building, in
neo-Romanesque style, on a Latin cross plan, dates from 1906. From the
southwest side rises a tower crowned with a pyramidal helmet; to the nave
adjoins a closed presbytery. The facade is decorated with an arcaded frieze. Behind the church
is the parish cemetery.
